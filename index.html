A small workshop with a slow way of working.

We started with a simple idea: a watch should still be worth owning in thirty years. That means movements that can be opened and repaired, cases that can be refinished, and straps that can be swapped in a minute.

We keep the range small and the details honest. Each model is finished, regulated and checked by hand before it leaves the workshop.

Mechanical movements

No batteries. Each watch runs on a mainspring and a balance wheel, the same principle watchmakers have refined for centuries.

Assembled by hand

Movements are fitted, oiled and regulated at the bench, then timed over several days before the case is closed.

Built to be serviced

Standard parts, clear service intervals and a workshop that will look after your watch long after you buy it.
